## Acceptance criteria and checks

| ID | Criterion | Check | Task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FR-1 | WHEN code fills an order, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep fill prices and average costs to 12 significant digits, EUR amounts to the cent and quantities to 1e-8, so a 100 EUR market round trip at the configured fees and slippage gives the same EUR result at BTC, ETH, USDC and at prices below 0.01 EUR | choose from: types, sonni, money-lab, runtime, build, sonni-e2e, deps | t1, t5 |
| FR-2 | WHEN a computed fill price, quantity or EUR amount is zero, negative, NaN or infinite, THE SYSTEM SHALL write no ledger, position or trade row, SHALL settle the order as rejected with a note, and SHALL record a broker incident in French | choose from: types, sonni, money-lab, runtime, build, sonni-e2e, deps | t1, t5 |
| FR-3 | WHEN one order cannot be processed, THE SYSTEM SHALL still process the other orders, the stops, the horizons and the daily snapshot, and a rejected stop SHALL leave the position with its stop level restored or an explicit incident | choose from: types, sonni, money-lab, runtime, build, sonni-e2e, deps | t1, t5 |
| FR-4 | WHEN a sale takes the whole position, THE SYSTEM SHALL leave exactly zero units, at any quantity size, and averaged buys, partial sales and full sales SHALL conserve units and euros (ledger, positions, trades and valuation agree) | choose from: types, sonni, money-lab, runtime, build, sonni-e2e, deps | t1, t5 |
| FR-5 | WHEN a stored position holds figures code cannot use, THE SYSTEM SHALL report it explicitly, SHALL show the portfolio total as unknown rather than a partial figure, and SHALL suspend buys, decisions and snapshots while the total cannot be computed, while sales and stops of valid positions keep working | choose from: types, sonni, money-lab, runtime, build, sonni-e2e, deps | t2, t5 |
| FR-6 | WHEN a unit price below 1 EUR is shown to the owner or the model, THE SYSTEM SHALL show at least 5 significant digits without exponent; prices from 1 EUR keep their current text | choose from: types, sonni, money-lab, runtime, build, sonni-e2e, deps | t3, t5 |
| FR-7 | THE SYSTEM SHALL provide a strictly read-only audit of a copy of the database (never the active file) that reports non-finite rows, zero prices and averaged positions whose cost drifted under cent rounding | choose from: types, sonni, money-lab, runtime, build, sonni-e2e, deps | t4, t5 |
| FR-8 | Strategy, order rules, configured fees and slippage, the universe (no price floor), decisions and their scoring, the SQLite schema, stored history and BTC/ETH results to the cent are unchanged; no paid call, no real trading | choose from: types, sonni, money-lab, runtime, build, sonni-e2e, deps | none yet |

## Research notes (read-only analysis of 2026-10-09, owner decisions of the same day)

- Measured on main (c1638c0) with throwaway probes: a 100 EUR market buy fills at round2(P x 1.0005). USDC 0.855
  fills at 0.86 (+0.58 %), USDC 0.8645 at 0.86 (below the market), ADA 0.212295 at 0.21 (a false open gain of
  +0.28 EUR), an asset at 0.0123 at 0.01 (-18.7 %, false gain +22.02 EUR). Below 0.005 EUR the fill price is 0,
  the quantity Infinity (stored as REAL 9e999, CHECK quantity >= 0 lets it through), avg_cost 0, equity Infinity;
  the next add or sale binds NaN as NULL and throws on every tick, so later orders, stops and the snapshot stop.
- Averaging below 10 EUR stores a cent-rounded avg_cost: USDC 0.86 then 0.869 gives a -0.58 EUR gap between the
  trades and the portfolio result; ADA 0.20 then 0.2149 a 2.70 EUR gap.
- No price floor exists: PUMPEUR (0.004885) and PEPEEUR (3.48e-6) passed the 250,000 EUR volume gate on
  2026-10-09; USDC (about 0.89 EUR) is a core asset.
- 12 significant digits (Number(v.toPrecision(12))) keep every BTC and ETH EUR figure of the existing tests
  identical. Over 20,000 random round trips per asset, sale proceeds (and so the result and the cash) move by
  exactly 1 cent in 0.1 % of BTC, 1.1 % of ETH, 1.05 % of PAXG and 10 % of stock round trips at 120-700 EUR,
  never more: the old proceeds used a fill price rounded to the cent, the new one is the exact figure.
- round8 is not idempotent from 2^25 (about 33.5 M) units: a sale of "all" re-rounded can exceed the stored
  quantity and fail CHECK quantity >= 0. The sale must take min(order, stored) without re-rounding.
- Owner decisions (2026-10-09): the configured 5 bps slippage applies at every price (USDC round trip -1.69
  instead of -1.59 EUR per 100 EUR); assets below 0.01 EUR allowed, no price floor; the new display only for
  prices below 1 EUR; capping aberrant Kraken spreads is deferred, but non-finite, zero or invalid values must be
  detected and handled without corruption; no historical repair or rewrite; no schema, fee or strategy change;
  a corrupt stored position is reported, the total is never shown as reliable, and the trading decisions that
  depend on it are suspended; the history audit is read-only and runs on a copy only.

## Global constraints

- Append-only tables are never rewritten; only pending orders may move to 'rejected' (already allowed by the
  schema). No SQLite schema change (no column, no trigger). Owner-facing text in French, code and docs in English.
- Tests make no network, inference or payment call. No paid wake is added (a rejected stop already wakes Sonni
  through the existing "orders settled by code" trigger).
